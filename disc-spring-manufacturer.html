What is a disc spring (Belleville spring)?

A disc spring, also called a Belleville spring or Belleville washer, is a shallow cone-shaped washer that acts as a spring when it is pressed flat. It carries a very high load for its size and deflects only a short distance, which makes it the usual choice where space along the axis is tight. Disc spring dimensions are commonly based on DIN 2093, so standard sizes are easy to match.

How disc springs are stacked

One disc gives a small deflection, so discs are stacked to get the load and travel you need.

Stack arrangementEffect
Series (discs facing opposite ways)Deflection adds up; the load stays the same as one disc
Parallel (discs facing the same way)Load adds up; the deflection stays the same as one disc, but friction between discs also rises
Series-parallelA combination used to reach a particular load and travel together

Do not press a disc past flat, and guide the stack on a rod or in a bore so it does not slip sideways.

Wave springs: the compact alternative

A wave spring is made from flat wire coiled with waves in it. It does the job of a coil spring in a much shorter height, so it fits bearing preload, clutch packs, seals and gearbox assemblies where a normal coil spring would be too tall. Multi-turn wave springs give a higher force and more travel in the same short length.

Disc, wave or coil spring?

TypeBest when
Disc springYou need the highest force in the smallest space and only a short stroke
Wave springYou need a modest force and a longer stroke in a short height, in a ring-shaped space
Coil (compression) springYou need a long stroke at a lower force and have the length for it — see our compression spring page

Where they are used

Valves and clutches, brakes, press tools and dies, bearing preload, gearbox and synchronizer assemblies, bolted joints (to keep bolt tension as parts settle) and safety devices.

Materials

Spring steel for general use, stainless steel where moisture or chemicals are present, and Inconel X-750 for high-temperature or very corrosive duty. See our material comparison.

Disc & Wave Spring Manufacturer FAQs

What is a Belleville spring?

A Belleville spring, also called a disc spring or Belleville washer, is a cone-shaped washer that works as a spring when it is flattened. It gives a high load in a very small axial space with a short stroke.

What is the price of a disc spring or Belleville washer?

How do I stack disc springs for more load or more travel?

Stack them facing the same way (parallel) to add load, or facing opposite ways (series) to add deflection. A combination of both reaches a given load and travel together.

What is the difference between a wave spring and a disc spring?

A disc spring gives a high force over a short stroke in a small space. A wave spring is a flat-wire coil with waves that gives a lower force over a longer stroke in a short, ring-shaped space.
