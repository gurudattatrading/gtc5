Fundamentals

What a Compression Spring Manufacturer Actually Does

A compression spring manufacturer is the company that turns raw spring wire into a finished, tested part as distinct from a distributor who buys finished springs and resells them. The work starts with selecting a wire grade suited to the load, environment and cycle life, then coiling it to the specified wire diameter, outer diameter, free length and number of coils.

After coiling, the spring is stress-relieved or heat-treated to set its shape and remove residual stress from winding. Ends are then squared or ground flat depending on how the spring will seat, and a finish plain, zinc-plated, powder-coated, or passivated for stainless steel is applied. A manufacturer that load-tests every batch, checking spring rate and solid height against the drawing before dispatch, is the one that saves an engineer from a failed installation later.

Because a compression spring looks like a simple part, most ordering mistakes happen at the specification stage, not the manufacturing stage which is exactly why a manufacturer who asks the right questions upfront is worth more than one who just quotes off a catalogue number.

Budgeting

What Decides Compression Spring Price in India

  • Material grade & wire diameter thicker wire and premium alloys cost more per kg and take longer to coil.
  • Coil count & free length more wire per spring means more material and machine time.
  • Surface finish plain finish is cheapest; zinc plating, powder coating or passivation each add a process step.
  • Custom tooling non-standard ends or pitch involve a one-time setup cost that amortises over larger runs.
  • Testing & documentation load testing, material certificates and inspection reports add cost on regulated orders.
  • Order quantity prototype pricing is always higher per piece than a bulk production run.

The only reliable way to get an exact number is to share your wire diameter, outer diameter, free length, coil count, material and quantity or send a sample or drawing over WhatsApp for a same-day estimate.

FAQ

Compression Spring Manufacturer FAQs

What makes a company a genuine compression spring manufacturer rather than a supplier?

A genuine manufacturer winds, heat-treats, finishes and load-tests the spring at its own facility. A supplier or trader buys finished springs from a manufacturer and resells them, usually with a markup and less control over customisation or lead time.

Can a compression spring manufacturer make custom sizes, or only standard catalogue springs?

A capable manufacturer can develop a spring from your drawing, a physical sample or a worn part, in addition to stocking standard catalogue sizes. Ask specifically whether custom tooling and prototype runs are supported.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for compression springs?

MOQ varies by manufacturer. Many, including direct factories, will quote single prototype springs as well as bulk industrial orders, though per-piece pricing is naturally higher at low quantities.

How is compression spring price calculated in India?

Price is driven by material grade, wire diameter, number of coils, surface finish, any custom tooling required, and order quantity, rather than spring type alone. Sharing exact specifications or a sample gives the most accurate quote.

Which material should I choose for a compression spring?

Mild steel suits general-purpose, cost-sensitive applications. Stainless steel (SS304/316) is preferred for corrosive, washdown or outdoor environments. Music wire and chrome vanadium suit high-strength or shock-load and high-temperature applications respectively.

Do compression spring manufacturers in Mumbai deliver outside Maharashtra?

Most established manufacturers dispatch PAN India by courier or transport, and manufacturers registered under an Import Export Code (IEC) can also export internationally.

How do I check if a compression spring will handle my required load?

Use a spring rate calculator with your wire diameter, outer diameter, free length and coil count to check spring rate, deflection and safe load before finalising an order, rather than relying on visual comparison to an existing part.

How long does it take to get a compression spring manufactured and delivered?

A typical process is a quote within 24 hours of sharing specifications, followed by sample approval for new or custom springs, then bulk production and dispatch. Exact timelines depend on quantity, material availability and whether tooling is needed.
