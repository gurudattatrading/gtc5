Conical and Barrel Compression Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. supplies conical and barrel-profile compression springs, supplied here as a 12-piece set covering multiple sizes and shapes.

Unlike a straight-sided cylindrical spring, conical and barrel springs nest down progressively under load and resist buckling far better under side loading — a useful property in compact assemblies where a standard spring would bow or bind.

Applications

  • Space-constrained mechanical assemblies
  • Seating, mattresses, and cushioning applications
  • Electrical contacts and connector springs
  • Equipment requiring buckle-resistant compression

Key Features

  • Buckle-resistant conical and barrel profiles
  • Nests down to a lower solid height than a straight spring
  • 12-piece set covering multiple sizes
  • Manufactured and supplied from Mumbai
