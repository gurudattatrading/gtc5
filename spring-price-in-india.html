What decides the price of a spring

FactorHow it affects the price
Wire diameter and wire lengthMore and thicker wire means more material and heavier machine work
MaterialMusic wire and hard drawn steel are the most economical. Stainless steel 304 and 316 cost more, and alloys such as Inconel X-750 cost the most
QuantityMachine set-up is spread over the batch, so the per-piece price falls as the quantity rises
Type and complexityExtension springs with special hooks and torsion springs with formed legs take more handling than a plain compression spring
Tolerance and testingTight tolerances and load testing at a stated height cost more than general tolerances
End grinding and heat treatmentGround ends and stress relieving add process steps
FinishZinc plating, passivation, powder coating or polishing are extra operations
Packing and deliveryIndividual packing, export packing and distance change the final cost

How much steel is in a spring? Weight calculator

The wire in a spring weighs less than most people expect. Wire length is about π × mean coil diameter × total coils, and weight is the wire cross-section area times that length times the density of the steel (about 7.85 g/cm³ for carbon steel and about 7.9 to 8.0 g/cm³ for stainless steel).

Worked example: a compression spring with 2 mm wire, 18 mm mean coil diameter and 8 coils uses about 452 mm of wire. That is about 1.42 cm³ of steel, or roughly 11 g. For a small spring like this the wire is a small part of the price, and machine time, heat treatment, finishing and set-up make up most of it. This is why quantity matters so much.

How to get a lower price without lowering quality

  • Choose a standard size. Our catalogue of 93 products has ready sizes that avoid special set-up.
  • Loosen tolerances that do not matter. Tighten only the dimensions and loads that affect function.
  • Order in larger or repeat batches. Set-up cost is shared across more pieces.
  • Match the material to the environment. Use stainless steel where moisture or chemicals demand it, and music wire or steel wire for dry indoor use. See the material comparison.
  • Skip finishes you do not need. A plain or oiled spring costs less than a plated one.
  • Send a complete drawing or sample. It avoids revisions and delays. See the quote checklist.

Spring price FAQs

How much does a custom spring cost in India?

It depends on the size, material, quantity, finish and tolerance. A small custom spring can cost a few rupees per piece and a large or alloy spring several hundred. Send your drawing or sample and we quote a per-piece price within 24 hours.

Why is a stainless steel spring more expensive than a steel spring?

Stainless steel wire costs more than music wire or steel wire, and it is a little harder to coil. Choose stainless steel when moisture or chemicals make it necessary.

Does ordering more springs reduce the price per piece?

Yes. Machine set-up is shared across the batch, so larger and repeat orders get a lower per-piece price.
