Mild Steel Extension Spring Set — Black and Silver Finish. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures extension springs in both black-oxide and bright silver finishes, giving customers a choice of appearance without compromising on strength or spring rate.

Extension springs work by resisting a pulling force and returning to their original length once the load is removed — ideal wherever a mechanism needs to stay closed, tensioned, or retracted under normal conditions. This 3-piece set is a convenient way to stock two finish options side by side.

Applications

  • Cabinet doors, hinges, and latching mechanisms
  • Garage doors and roller shutters
  • Automotive and two-wheeler assemblies
  • General industrial tensioning applications

Key Features

  • Consistent extension rate for predictable performance
  • Available in black-oxide or silver (zinc-plated) finish
  • Custom coil diameter, wire gauge, and hook style available
