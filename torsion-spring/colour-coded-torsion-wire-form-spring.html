Torsion Wire Form Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures torsion springs with formed wire-form legs, colour-coated so operators can identify the correct spring rate at a glance without checking a reference chart.

Torsion springs store and release rotational (twisting) force, and custom-formed legs let the spring interface directly with the mating part — hinges, levers, and latching mechanisms all rely on this kind of precision leg forming.

Applications

  • Levers and rotational assemblies
  • Assembly lines needing quick visual grade identification
