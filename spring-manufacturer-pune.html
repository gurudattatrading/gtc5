Our Range

Types of Springs We Manufacture

We manufacture the full range of mechanical springs in-house, from single prototypes to bulk production runs.

Compression Springs
Absorb and store energy under a pushing load the most common spring type across machinery.
Extension (Tension) Springs
Resist a pulling force and return to original length once released.
Torsion Springs
Store energy through twisting rather than linear movement used in hinges, levers and clamps.
Die Springs
Heavy-duty compression springs built for tooling and press applications.
Disc / Belleville Springs
Stacked conical washers that deliver a high load in a very small space.
Wire Forms
Custom-bent wire components shaped to a specific drawing rather than a standard coil.
Circlips & Snap Rings
Retaining rings that hold components in place on a shaft or housing.
Hooks & Pins
Supporting hardware manufactured alongside spring orders.
Custom & Non-Standard
Built to your drawing, sample, or reverse-engineered from a worn part.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a spring rate and why does it matter?

Spring rate (k) is the force required to compress or extend a spring by one unit of length, expressed as F = k × x. It determines how stiff a spring feels in use, and getting it wrong is the most common reason a replacement spring doesn't perform like the original.

Which parts of Pune do you deliver springs to?

Pune city (Kothrud, Hadapsar, Camp, Viman Nagar, Wagholi) and the auto-industrial belt — Chakan, Bhosari, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Talegaon and Ranjangaon. Share your location on WhatsApp for a dispatch estimate.

Can I get a custom spring made from just a sample, without a drawing?

Yes. Send the worn or broken sample along with a brief usage description on WhatsApp, and the team can work out the dimensions and recommend a suitable material grade.

Do you supply single/prototype springs to Pune, or only bulk OEM orders?

Both — from a single replacement spring for a repair shop to bulk OEM production runs for auto-ancillary units in Chakan and Pimpri-Chinchwad. There's no fixed minimum order quantity.

What material holds up best for Pune's auto-component and machine-tool applications?

Chrome Vanadium (ASTM A232) is generally preferred for the shock-load, high-cycle conditions typical of automotive clutch, valve and suspension springs, while Stainless Steel (ASTM A313) suits pharma and washdown environments.

What information is needed to quote a custom spring?

Ideally wire diameter, outer diameter, free length, number of coils, material and end type — the same inputs used in the free Spring Rate Calculator. A drawing or physical sample works just as well if exact dimensions aren't available.
