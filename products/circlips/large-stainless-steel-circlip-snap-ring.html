About Large Stainless Steel Circlip (Snap Ring)

Large Stainless Steel Circlip Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures large-diameter internal circlips (snap rings) for retaining bearings and components inside bored housings on heavier machinery, where standard small circlips aren't rated for the load or bore size.

Made from stainless steel for long service life and corrosion resistance, these circlips are precision-formed to seat cleanly in the retaining groove without deforming under normal operating loads.

Applications

  • Large bearing housings and gearboxes
  • Heavy industrial and off-road machinery
  • Marine and outdoor equipment (corrosion resistance)
  • Custom OEM assemblies requiring large-bore retention
