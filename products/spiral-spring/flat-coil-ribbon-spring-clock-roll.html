Flat Ribbon Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures flat, ribbon-section springs wound into a compact coil, clock-spring style, for applications that need rotational energy storage in a very small package.

The flat cross-section allows the spring to coil tightly around itself, storing more usable energy per unit volume than a round-wire spring of similar size — a key reason it's the standard construction used inside retractable reels and mechanical timing devices.

Applications

  • Retractable cord and tape reels
  • Mechanical timers and clockwork devices
  • Compact rotational energy-storage assemblies
  • Custom OEM ribbon-spring requirements

Key Features

  • Compact coil for space-constrained assemblies
  • Efficient rotational energy storage
  • Consistent unwind torque
