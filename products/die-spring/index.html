Die Spring Essentials

Die springs are heavy-duty rectangular-wire compression springs for press tools, dies and moulds, graded by duty level (light to extra-heavy). Colour coding varies by manufacturer — see our die spring colour code chart for how to match by dimensions and duty rather than colour alone.

What we need to quote: hole/bore diameter, rod diameter, free length, required duty level or working load, and quantity.

Planning a custom order?

When selecting die springs for an assembly, start with the function, fitting dimensions, material or grade, operating environment and required quantity. For springs, include load or deflection, wire diameter, outside diameter, free length, coil count and end style when known. For pins, clips, hooks, seals and wire forms, a drawing or sample plus mating-part measurements helps avoid assumptions.

For a reliable quote, share expected duty cycle, finish or coating, tolerance needs, and any corrosion, temperature or vibration exposure. We can review the information and confirm material availability, manufacturability and lead time for the specific design. Final dimensions and performance requirements should be agreed against the approved drawing or sample before production.
