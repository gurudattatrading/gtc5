Spiral Clock Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures spiral clock springs in a range of sizes, supplied here as an assortment for engineers and repair technicians who need to match an existing spring quickly.

Clock springs store rotational energy as they're wound and release it in a controlled manner as they unwind — the same working principle used in retractable cord reels, seatbelt retractors, and self-closing mechanisms.

Applications

  • Retractable cord and cable reels
  • Seatbelt retractors and self-closing mechanisms
  • Timing devices and clockwork mechanisms
  • General rotational energy-storage applications
