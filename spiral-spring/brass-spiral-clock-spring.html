Brass Spiral Clock Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures spiral clock springs in brass for applications where corrosion resistance and smooth, quiet unwind action matter more than raw torque output.

Brass doesn't rust and has excellent fatigue resistance for a non-ferrous material, making it a popular choice in instruments, retractable mechanisms, and decorative or precision applications where a steel spring's finish or magnetic properties would be a drawback.

Applications

  • Precision instruments and retractable mechanisms
  • Applications requiring non-magnetic properties
  • Corrosion-prone or humid environments
  • Decorative and specialty clockwork mechanisms
