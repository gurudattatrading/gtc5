Custom Spring Cost: What Affects the Price and What to Send for a Quote

Quick answer: Custom spring price depends mainly on wire material and size, spring type and complexity, quantity, tolerances and testing, and finishing — send full specifications to get an accurate quote.

By Gurudatta Trading Co., Mumbai · Updated 2026-09-28

What affects the price

  • Wire material and diameter: stainless steel and thicker wire cost more than thin carbon steel wire.
  • Spring type and ends: ground ends, hooks, legs and special forms add operations.
  • Quantity: setup time is spread over more pieces in larger batches, so unit cost usually falls.
  • Tolerances and load testing: tight tolerances or load-tested springs need more inspection.
  • Finishing: zinc plating, powder coating, passivation or oiling add cost.
  • Packing and delivery: location and packing needs influence the final quote.

Quote checklist

  1. Spring type (compression, extension, torsion or other)
  2. Wire diameter, outer diameter, free length and number of coils — see how to measure a spring
  3. Material and finish
  4. Load or working requirement
  5. Quantity and delivery location
  6. Drawing, sample or photos
