The Process

5 Steps From Enquiry to Delivery

The same process applies whether you're ordering one prototype spring or a bulk industrial batch.

1
Tell Us What You Need
Share a drawing, a physical sample, or working parameters (wire diameter, outer diameter, free length, load) over WhatsApp, email or phone. No exact spec yet? Use the free Spring Rate Calculator to work one out first.
2
Get a Quote Within 24 Hours
The team reviews your requirement and material options, then replies with pricing and lead time usually the same day.
3
Confirm & We Manufacture
Once confirmed, springs are wound, heat-treated and finished in-house to your exact tolerance, in Mild Steel, Stainless Steel, Music Wire or other alloys on request.
4
Every Batch Is Quality Checked
Dimensions, load and finish are checked against your spec before packing part of how the ISO 9001:2015 certification is maintained.
5
PAN-India Dispatch, Export on Request
Orders ship across India, and being IEC-registered, export shipments outside India are handled as well.

Common Questions

FAQ Ordering Process

Do I need exact spring dimensions before contacting you?

No a description, a photo of the old part, or a rough sketch is enough to start. The team will help pin down exact wire diameter, coil diameter, free length and load, or you can work it out yourself first using the free Spring Rate Calculator.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No fixed MOQ. Orders range from a single prototype spring to bulk industrial quantities.

Can you manufacture from just a physical sample, without a drawing?

Yes. A sample spring, or even a worn or broken part, can be measured and reverse-engineered to manufacture a matching replacement.

How is the price worked out?

Price depends on material, wire diameter, coil count, finish and order quantity. Exact pricing is confirmed in the quote after the requirement is reviewed.

Do you deliver outside Maharashtra or outside India?

Yes. Dispatch is available PAN India, and the company is IEC-registered for export orders outside India.

What if the online calculator can't generate pricing for my design?

Send the specification sheet from the calculator on WhatsApp and the team will review it manually and reply with options this happens for unusual sizes or combinations the calculator isn't tuned for.
