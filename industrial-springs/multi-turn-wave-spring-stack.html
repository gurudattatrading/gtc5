Multi-Turn Wave Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Where a single-turn wave spring can't deliver enough force, Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures multi-turn wave springs — several waves stacked in series within one continuous coil — to raise the spring force while keeping the axial length short.

This construction is popular in compact bearing and clutch assemblies where engineers need more force than a single wave can provide but still can't spare the space for a full coil spring.

Applications

  • High-force bearing preload assemblies
  • Compact clutch and brake packs
  • Precision instruments and actuators
  • Space-constrained OEM designs
