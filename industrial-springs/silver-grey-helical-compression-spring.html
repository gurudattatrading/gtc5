Helical Compression Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. produces silver-grey finish helical compression springs, close-coiled for stable, repeatable performance under axial load.

Helical compression springs are the most common spring type used across Indian industry — from valves and actuators to machine guides and general equipment — because their simple coil geometry is easy to manufacture to tight tolerance and easy to integrate into an existing assembly.

Applications

  • Industrial valves and pneumatic/hydraulic actuators
  • Machine guides, presses, and general equipment
  • Automotive and engineering components
  • OEM assemblies requiring a standard compression spring
