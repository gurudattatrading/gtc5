Stainless Steel Disc Spring (Belleville Washer) Manufacturer in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. supplies SS disc springs designed to deliver very high spring force from an extremely small axial footprint — ideal wherever a coil spring simply won't fit.

Disc springs can be stacked in series to increase deflection, in parallel to increase load capacity, or in a mixed configuration to fine-tune the load-deflection curve for a specific application. Stainless steel construction keeps them working reliably even in corrosive or washdown environments.

Applications

  • Bearing preload and clamping assemblies
  • Valves, couplings, and safety relief mechanisms
  • Bolted joint load retention
  • Food, pharma, and marine equipment (corrosion resistance)

Key Features

  • High load capacity in a compact axial space
  • Corrosion-resistant stainless steel construction
  • Configurable stack (series/parallel) for custom force curves
  • Manufactured and supplied in bulk from Mumbai
