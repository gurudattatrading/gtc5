Wave Spring Manufacturer in Mumbai. Wave springs from Gurudatta Trading Co. deliver spring force in roughly a third of the axial space needed by a standard coil spring, making them the preferred choice wherever engineers are fighting for space inside a housing.

Because the wave profile flattens under load in a controlled, predictable way, these springs are widely used for preload, end-play take-up, and vibration damping in rotating and reciprocating assemblies.

Applications

  • Bearing preload in motors, pumps, and gearboxes
  • Clutch packs and transmission assemblies
  • Seals, connectors, and electrical contacts
  • Vibration and noise damping
