Stainless Steel Belleville Disc Spring Supplier in Mumbai. Gurudatta Trading Co. manufactures SS Belleville (conical) disc springs that deliver a high spring force in a very small axial footprint, supplied here as a matched 2-piece set.

The conical, dished shape means the spring flattens progressively as load increases — giving engineers a load-deflection curve that a simple coil spring can't replicate in the same space. Stainless steel construction adds corrosion resistance for humid, washdown, or marine environments.

Applications

  • Bolted-joint load retention
  • Marine, food-processing, and chemical equipment
  • Bearing and coupling preload
