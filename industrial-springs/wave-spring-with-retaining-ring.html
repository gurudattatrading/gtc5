Wave Spring with Matching Retaining Ring — supplied together by Gurudatta Trading Co. so engineers get a matched pair sized for direct drop-in replacement, without needing to source the ring separately.

This combination is common in bearing and gearbox assemblies where the wave spring provides axial preload and the retaining ring locks the assembly in place, keeping the whole stack under consistent, controlled tension.

Applications

  • Automotive and industrial gear trains
  • Replacement/spare-parts stock for maintenance teams
