How to use these problems

These ten problems cover Hooke's law, spring constant, springs in series and parallel, energy, work and oscillation, as taught in Class 11 and 12 physics, IGCSE and A-level courses. Use g = 9.8 m/s². Try each one before reading the solution. The formulas are on our Hooke's law page.

No.ProblemSolution
1A 2 kg mass hung on a spring stretches it by 0.04 m. Find the spring constant.F = mg = 2 × 9.8 = 19.6 N. k = F ÷ x = 19.6 ÷ 0.04 = 490 N/m
2A spring with k = 250 N/m is pulled by 50 N. Find the extension.x = F ÷ k = 50 ÷ 250 = 0.2 m
3What force stretches a spring with k = 800 N/m by 5 cm?F = kx = 800 × 0.05 = 40 N
4Two springs, k₁ = 300 N/m and k₂ = 600 N/m, are joined end to end (series). Find the combined constant.1/k = 1/300 + 1/600 = 3/600, so k = 200 N/m
5The same two springs are connected side by side (parallel). Find the combined constant.k = 300 + 600 = 900 N/m
6A spring with k = 400 N/m is stretched 0.1 m. Find the energy stored.U = ½kx² = 0.5 × 400 × 0.1² = 2 J
7How much work is done stretching the same spring from 0.1 m to 0.2 m?W = ½k(x₂² − x₁²) = 0.5 × 400 × (0.04 − 0.01) = 6 J
8A graph of force against extension is a straight line through (0.05 m, 2 N) and (0.15 m, 6 N). Find k.Slope = (6 − 2) ÷ (0.15 − 0.05) = 40 N/m
9A 0.5 kg mass oscillates on a spring with k = 200 N/m. Find the time period.T = 2π√(m ÷ k) = 2π√(0.5 ÷ 200) = 2π × 0.05 = 0.314 s
10A spring of constant 120 N/m is cut into two equal halves. Find the constant of each half.A shorter spring is stiffer. Each half has 240 N/m

Key points to remember

  • Hooke's law, F = kx, works only within the elastic limit.
  • In parallel the constants add. In series the reciprocals add.
  • The area under a force-extension graph is the work done, which equals ½kx² for a straight line.
  • The time period of a mass on a spring depends on the mass and k, and not on the amplitude.
  • Always convert to SI units (metres, newtons) before calculating.

Hooke's law problems: FAQs

How do you solve a Hooke's law problem?

Convert to SI units, then use F = kx. Find the force from the weight (F = mg) if a mass is hanging, and rearrange for the unknown: x = F ÷ k or k = F ÷ x.

What is the spring constant of two springs in series?

The reciprocals add: 1/k = 1/k₁ + 1/k₂. For 300 N/m and 600 N/m the combined constant is 200 N/m.

What happens to the spring constant if a spring is cut in half?

Each half has twice the original spring constant, because a shorter spring is stiffer.

How is work done on a spring calculated?

The work stretching a spring from x₁ to x₂ is ½k(x₂² − x₁²), and from rest it is ½kx².

What is the time period of a mass on a spring?

T = 2π√(m ÷ k), which depends on the mass and the spring constant and not on the amplitude.
