Stainless Steel R-Pin Manufacturer in Mumbai. Also known as a hairpin cotter pin, the R-pin from Gurudatta Trading Co. is designed for fast, tool-free locking of clevis and hitch pins — simply push it through the pin's cross-hole and it stays locked in place until manually removed.

Made from stainless steel for corrosion resistance, this R-pin is a workhorse component across agricultural, marine, and general mechanical assemblies wherever a quick-release, no-tool locking pin is required.

Applications

  • Clevis and hitch pin locking
  • Agricultural machinery and implements
  • Marine and outdoor equipment
  • General mechanical fastening
